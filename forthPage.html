Mill House: A Historic Large Holiday Cottage near York

Mill House is a one-of-a-kind North Yorkshire holiday cottage for families and groups who want character, space and time together. Sleeping up to 10 guests, or 12 including a sofa bed, the five-bedroom property combines dramatic historic details with an open, sociable layout and the comforts of a modern group stay.

Exposed timbers date back to 1765, while arrow slits, vaulted ceilings and a double-height barn window tell the story of the building's past. Outside, a private south-facing garden and hot tub provide a quiet place for an evening drink, a barbecue or a long soak after a day exploring York and the surrounding countryside.

Historic Character, Made for Modern Group Stays

Mill House has the sort of details that cannot be recreated in a new build. The stone exterior, original openings, aged beams and soaring window give it a real sense of place, while the interior has been arranged around the way families and groups actually spend time together.

The open-plan ground floor means the cooks are not shut away from the conversation. Meals can be prepared, drinks poured and plans made while everyone shares the same generous living space. On cooler evenings, the log burner adds warmth and atmosphere; in summer, the garden becomes an extra room.

Upstairs, the galleried landing looks across the main space and leads to well-proportioned bedrooms. The largest bedroom has a super-king bed, walk-in wardrobe and en-suite bathroom, making it a particularly comfortable choice for a couple celebrating a wedding or special occasion.

Charming Countryside Stays

Five Bedrooms for Families and Friends

With five bedrooms and three bathrooms, Mill House gives larger groups a practical balance of togetherness and privacy. Two bedrooms have en-suite facilities, while two further upstairs rooms share the large house bathroom. The additional sleeping arrangement on the sofa bed takes the maximum occupancy to 12.

If bedroom configuration or mobility is important for your group, speak to Sue before booking. She can help you check the current layout and whether Mill House, The Byre or The Stables best suits your party.

Private Hot Tub, Pool and Pizza Shack

The private south-facing garden is directly connected to the cottage and includes a hot tub, giving your group an easy place to relax without leaving the property. The garden can also connect with The Byre's outdoor space when both cottages are booked together.

Mill House guests share use of Birdforth Hall's heated indoor swimming pool on a rota basis, along with the pool table and table tennis. The Pizza Shack adds another ready-made group evening: use the wood-fired Igneus pizza oven, with firelighters, kindling and logs provided for the experience.

Book Mill House with The Byre for Up to 22 Guests

For a bigger gathering, Mill House adjoins The Byre and the two properties can be linked. Together they provide nine bedrooms and accommodation for up to 22 guests, with large kitchens, dining areas, lounges, private gardens and hot tubs.

This arrangement works especially well for multi-generational family holidays, milestone celebrations and wedding groups. Everyone can be close, while the two cottages still provide separate spaces for quieter moments.

Village Charm

Explore York, Thirsk and Herriot Country

Birdforth Hall is around 20 minutes north of York and within easy reach of Thirsk, Easingwold and the wider landscapes of North Yorkshire. Spend a day exploring York's historic centre, discover the market towns and villages of Herriot Country, or travel farther into the North York Moors and Yorkshire Dales.

When you return, there is no need to organise another activity. Light the log burner, open the garden doors, book a pool session or gather everyone in the Pizza Shack for an easy evening together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Birdforth Hall?

−

Birdforth Hall is in Birdforth, North Yorkshire, between York and Thirsk. It is around 20 minutes north of York, outside Easingwold, with convenient access to the A19.

How many guests can stay at Birdforth Hall?

+

Across our four holiday cottages—Mill House, The Granary, The Stables, and The Byre—Birdforth Hall can accommodate up to 43 guests in total.

Does every cottage have a hot tub?

+

Yes, every cottage has its own private garden and hot tub for your exclusive use throughout your stay.

Is there a swimming pool?

+

Yes, we have a heated indoor swimming pool. Each cottage enjoys exclusive use of the pool on a scheduled rota basis.

Are the cottages dog friendly?

+

Yes, all our holiday cottages are dog friendly so you can bring your four-legged family members along to enjoy the North Yorkshire countryside.

Do you accept hen or stag parties?

+

We welcome family celebrations and respectful group gatherings. Please get in touch with us in advance to discuss the details of your stay.
