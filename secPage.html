THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

The Forge: A Barn Wedding Venue with a Story

The Forge was once a working blacksmith's forge and has been carefully restored as a light, welcoming wedding barn. For nearly 200 years, blacksmiths were connected with local wedding traditions: couples were married over the anvil and the hammer strike announced a new marriage to the village.

Today, the building keeps that sense of history while giving couples the comfort and flexibility of a modern venue. Natural textures, warm lighting, fairy lights and lanterns create an inviting atmosphere, while the open views and grounds offer varied backdrops for photographs throughout the day.

The Forge works beautifully for a seated wedding breakfast, speeches and evening celebrations, but it never feels as though you have to follow a fixed formula. It is a characterful starting point for your ideas..

Wedding Ceremonies

Indoor and Outdoor Wedding Ceremoniese

You can hold your ceremony inside The Forge or outdoors on the south-facing lawn. Inside, the barn offers warmth, natural light and panoramic countryside views. Outside, the lawns and surrounding fields provide an open-air setting with the relaxed feel of a rural Yorkshire wedding.

There are also picturesque churches nearby for couples planning a religious ceremony, including St Mary's Church in Birdforth. You can then return to Birdforth Hall for drinks, your wedding breakfast and the evening celebration.

Whichever option you choose, the team will help you think through timings, layout and the practical details, including a comfortable alternative if the weather changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Birdforth Hall?

−

Birdforth Hall is in Birdforth, North Yorkshire, between York and Thirsk. It is around 20 minutes north of York, outside Easingwold, with convenient access to the A19.

How many guests can stay at Birdforth Hall?

+

Across our four holiday cottages—Mill House, The Granary, The Stables, and The Byre—Birdforth Hall can accommodate up to 43 guests in total.

Does every cottage have a hot tub?

+

Yes, every cottage has its own private garden and hot tub for your exclusive use throughout your stay.

Is there a swimming pool?

+

Yes, we have a heated indoor swimming pool. Each cottage enjoys exclusive use of the pool on a scheduled rota basis.

Are the cottages dog friendly?

+

Yes, all our holiday cottages are dog friendly so you can bring your four-legged family members along to enjoy the North Yorkshire countryside.

Do you accept hen or stag parties?

+

We welcome family celebrations and respectful group gatherings. Please get in touch with us in advance to discuss the details of your stay.
