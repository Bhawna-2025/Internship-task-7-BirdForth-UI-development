THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

A Relaxed Wedding Venue in North Yorkshire

At Birdforth Hall, your wedding begins the moment you arrive. With onsite accommodation, open countryside views and a relaxed North Yorkshire setting, family and friends can gather the night before, settle into the cottages, enjoy food and drinks, and start making memories before the ceremony itself.

The Forge is our exclusive-use barn wedding venue between York and Thirsk. Historic, warm and full of character, it gives you the freedom to plan a day that feels like yours, with indoor and outdoor ceremony spaces, flexible reception areas and personal support from a family-run team.

This is a wedding venue for couples who want time together, not just a timetable. Your closest people can stay onsite, the venue is yours to enjoy before, during and after the day, and the celebration has room to breathe.

An Exclusive-Use Country Wedding Venue near York and Thirsk

Birdforth Hall sits in peaceful parkland and open farmland close to York, Thirsk and Easingwold. It feels private and tucked away, yet it is only a short journey from the main road and rail network, making it practical for guests travelling from across Yorkshire, the North East, the Midlands and beyond.

The converted barns, lawns, mature trees and countryside views create a naturally relaxed atmosphere. There is no hotel conveyor belt and you will NEVER SHARE the venue with another wedding. Exclusive hire means the spaces are yours, the people around you are your people, and the day can unfold in a way that feels unhurried and personal.

Your Wedding Starts the Day Before

Access to The Forge from 12.30pm the day before your wedding is included as standard. That gives you time to decorate, meet suppliers, add personal details and settle into the cottages without squeezing everything into the wedding morning.

It also gives your guests the chance to arrive properly. Share an informal meal, open a bottle, make pizzas, enjoy the hot tubs or take a swim in the indoor pool. With laid-back music, good food and familiar faces gathering in one place, families and friendship groups can meet before the big day begins.
