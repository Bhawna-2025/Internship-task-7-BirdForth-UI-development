THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

Luxury Holiday Cottages near York for Families and Groups

Birdforth Hall is made for the kind of break where everyone can stay together and still have room to breathe. Set in the North Yorkshire countryside between York and Thirsk, our four spacious holiday cottages combine converted-barn character with private gardens, hot tubs and shared leisure facilities.

Choose one cottage for a family holiday or bring several generations and friendship groups together across the wider site. With accommodation for up to 43 guests in total, Birdforth Hall works especially well for birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, wedding stays and relaxed countryside escapes.

Each property has its own personality, from the historic timbers of Mill House to the single-storey layout of The Stables. What they share is generous living space, well-equipped kitchens and the little extras that give a group more ways to enjoy the stay.

Charming Countryside Stays

More Than a Place to Sleep

A group holiday is often remembered for the time between the days out: breakfast around a big table, children heading to the pool, pizzas coming out of the oven and everyone meeting back in the hot tub as the evening settles in.

At Birdforth Hall, the facilities are designed to make those moments easier. Cottage guests can enjoy:

A private garden and hot tub with every cottage.

Shared use of the heated indoor swimming pool on a rota basis.

A pool table and table tennis.

The DIY Pizza Shack with a wood-fired pizza oven.

Ample free parking close to the properties.

Superfast Wi-Fi in every cottage.

Travel cots, highchairs, a baby bath and changing mats when required.

Up to two well-behaved dogs in each cottage for an additional charge.

You can plan every day or barely leave the grounds. Both approaches work here

Large Group Accommodation in North Yorkshire

Birdforth Hall is a practical base for groups because the cottages offer a mix of shared and private space. Families can eat and relax together, while separate bedrooms, bathrooms and gardens give everyone room when they need it.

For a larger single group, Mill House and The Byre can be opened together to provide nine bedrooms and accommodation for up to 22 guests. For bigger gatherings, multiple cottages can be booked across the site, subject to availability and the nature of your stay.

We welcome family celebrations, milestone birthdays, reunions, wedding groups and peaceful retreats. We do not accept stag or hen party bookings.

Yorkshire Escapes

Dog-Friendly Cottages near York

Dogs are part of the family for many of our guests, so each cottage welcomes up to two well-behaved dogs for an additional charge. Every property has a private garden, and The Stables is particularly useful for dog owners thanks to its large enclosed lawn.

The surrounding countryside offers plenty of opportunities for fresh air and local walks. Please keep dogs under control around livestock and do not leave them unattended in a cottage unless they are safely crated.

Village Charm

Explore York, Thirsk and Herriot Country

Birdforth Hall is around 20 minutes north of York and within easy reach of Thirsk, Easingwold and the wider landscapes of North Yorkshire. Spend a day exploring York's historic centre, discover the market towns and villages of Herriot Country, or travel farther into the North York Moors and Yorkshire Dales.

When you return, there is no need to organise another activity. Light the log burner, open the garden doors, book a pool session or gather everyone in the Pizza Shack for an easy evening together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Birdforth Hall?

−

Birdforth Hall is in Birdforth, North Yorkshire, between York and Thirsk. It is around 20 minutes north of York, outside Easingwold, with convenient access to the A19.

How many guests can stay at Birdforth Hall?

+

Across our four holiday cottages—Mill House, The Granary, The Stables, and The Byre—Birdforth Hall can accommodate up to 43 guests in total.

Does every cottage have a hot tub?

+

Yes, every cottage has its own private garden and hot tub for your exclusive use throughout your stay.

Is there a swimming pool?

+

Yes, we have a heated indoor swimming pool. Each cottage enjoys exclusive use of the pool on a scheduled rota basis.

Are the cottages dog friendly?

+

Yes, all our holiday cottages are dog friendly so you can bring your four-legged family members along to enjoy the North Yorkshire countryside.

Do you accept hen or stag parties?

+

We welcome family celebrations and respectful group gatherings. Please get in touch with us in advance to discuss the details of your stay.
