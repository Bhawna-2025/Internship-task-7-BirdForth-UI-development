THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

Birdforth Hall Holiday Cottages and Wedding Venue near York

A peaceful countryside place to relax and comes together near York, Thirsk & Easingwold.

At Birdforth Hall, first impressions matter. From the moment you arrive, surrounded by open parkland views, converted barns, friendly animals and quiet North Yorkshire setting are here to help you feel that you have made the right choice. Whether you are planning a wedding, a family celebration, a countryside break or a group stay, Birdforth Hall gives you space to slow down, enjoy good company and make proper memories together.

Your wedding begins the moment you arrive. With onsite accommodation, open countryside views and a relaxed North Yorkshire setting, family and friends can gather the night before, settle into the cottages, enjoy food, drinks, the hot tubs, pool and our Pizza Shack.

You and your family can start making memories before the big day itself. It is a warm, easy and personal way to bring everyone together, giving your wedding more time, more meaning and a proper sense of celebration.

THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

An Exclusive-Use Barn Wedding
Venue in North Yorkshire

For weddings, The Forge at Birdforth Hall offers an exclusive-use barn venue with a relaxed countryside feel. Originally a working blacksmith's forge, the space has been carefully brought back to life as a characterful wedding venue with natural light, rustic details and room to make the day your own.

Couples can choose from indoor and outdoor ceremony options, a celebration in the barn, drinks outside on the lawn, or a more relaxed reception in the Mediterranean Terrace Room, where floor-to-ceiling glass doors look out towards the gardens, outdoor ceremony spaces, mature trees and surrounding fields.

Your wedding should feel like you, not like a fixed formula.

That is why Birdforth Hall is built around flexibility, exclusive use and a simple promise: your wedding, your way

A Countryside Setting Within Easy Reach of York

Birdforth Hall is around 20 minutes north of York, outside Easingwold and on the way to Thirsk. The rural setting feels calm and private, while road and rail connections make it practical for guests travelling from across Yorkshire and farther afield.

From here, you can explore York, Thirsk and Herriot Country, plan days out across the North York Moors and Yorkshire Dales, or simply stay close to home and enjoy the gardens, animals and facilities onsite. Our long-horn cattle graze in the fields, horses like Revel are part of daily life, and Bella the pig is often the guest favourite.

About us

Family-Run, Yorkshire-Born

Birdforth Hall is run by Sue and Paul, with a genuine love of rural life, the local area and bringing people together. We live onsite and are on hand when you need us, whether you would like a local recommendation, help planning a group stay or practical support for your wedding.

You will have your own space, but you will never feel as though you have been handed a key and forgotten. It is a simple approach: a warm welcome, well-prepared accommodation and thoughtful help that makes a stay feel easy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Birdforth Hall?

−

Birdforth Hall is in Birdforth, North Yorkshire, between York and Thirsk. It is around 20 minutes north of York, outside Easingwold, with convenient access to the A19.

How many guests can stay at Birdforth Hall?

+

Across our four holiday cottages—Mill House, The Granary, The Stables, and The Byre—Birdforth Hall can accommodate up to 43 guests in total.

Does every cottage have a hot tub?

+

Yes, every cottage has its own private garden and hot tub for your exclusive use throughout your stay.

Is there a swimming pool?

+

Yes, we have a heated indoor swimming pool. Each cottage enjoys exclusive use of the pool on a scheduled rota basis.

Are the cottages dog friendly?

+

Yes, all our holiday cottages are dog friendly so you can bring your four-legged family members along to enjoy the North Yorkshire countryside.

Do you accept hen or stag parties?

+

We welcome family celebrations and respectful group gatherings. Please get in touch with us in advance to discuss the details of your stay.
