THE FORGE • BIRDFORTH HALL

Birdforth Hall Holiday Cottages and Wedding Venue near York

A peaceful countryside place to relax and comes together near York, Thirsk & Easingwold.

At Birdforth Hall, first impressions matter. From the moment you arrive, surrounded by open parkland views, converted barns, friendly animals and quiet North Yorkshire setting are here to help you feel that you have made the right choice. Whether you are planning a wedding, a family celebration, a countryside break or a group stay, Birdforth Hall gives you space to slow down, enjoy good company and make proper memories together.

Your wedding begins the moment you arrive. With onsite accommodation, open countryside views and a relaxed North Yorkshire setting, family and friends can gather the night before, settle into the cottages, enjoy food, drinks, the hot tubs, pool and our Pizza Shack.

You and your family can start making memories before the big day itself. It is a warm, easy and personal way to bring everyone together, giving your wedding more time, more meaning and a proper sense of celebration.

STAY WITH US

Luxury holiday cottages
for families and groups

Our four dog-friendly holiday cottages are designed for groups who want to spend proper time together without giving up comfort or personal space. Across Mill House, The Granary, The Stables and The Byre, there is accommodation for up to 43 guests, with generous kitchens, sociable living areas and bedrooms suited to families and mixed-age groups.

Dog-friendly cottages

Every cottage has its own private garden and hot tub. Each cottage has exclusive use of the heated indoor pool on a rota basis. We also have a pool table, table tennis and DIY Pizza Shack, for your use during your stay. There is ample onsite parking, superfast Wi-Fi and thoughtful family equipment including travel cots, highchairs, a baby bath and changing mats.

Choose a detached cottage with long-horn cattle looking over the gate, a single-storey stay with parking beside the door, or two adjoining properties that can sleep up to 22 guests together. However your group is made up, there is space to cook, eat, celebrate and relax at your own pace.
